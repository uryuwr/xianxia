# 关卡设计 · 参考资料

## 书
- Christopher W. Totten《An Architectural Approach to Level Design》（CRC 2014；第 2 版 2019）。空间的三种尺度（狭窄 = 紧张、私密 = 安心、开阔 = 暴露）、prospect / refuge、门槛与到达、Figure-Ground。https://www.corentin.xyz/book/architectural-level-design.html ；https://books.google.com/books/about/Architectural_Approach_to_Level_Design.html?id=PQqWDwAAQBAJ
- The Level Design Book · Disneyland：weenie 地标按顺序把人拉过去。https://book.leveldesignbook.com/studies/irl/disneyland
- Theory of Theme Parks · Weenie。http://theoryofthemeparks.blogspot.com/2015/08/wayfinding-in-themed-design-weenie.html

## GDC 演讲与文章
- GDC Level Design Workshop: Pacing。节奏 = 活动 × 强度 × 时长；Half-Life 2 的 5 种节奏元素。https://www.gdcvault.com/play/mediaProxy.php?sid=1022451 ；GDC China 2012 版 https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/level-design-workshop-gdc-china-2012/15306714
- Smith & Worch "What Happened Here? Environmental Storytelling"（GDC 2010）。让玩家自己拼出发生过的事，同时有导航和预告作用。https://gdcvault.com/play/1012647/What-Happened-Here-Environmental ；讲者笔记 https://www.worch.com/files/gdc/What_Happened_Here_Web_Notes.pdf
- Scott Rogers 的 GDC 2009 分享（Boing Boing 整理）。https://boingboing.net/2009/03/27/gamedesign-lessons-f.html
- Trinity Part 6 · Intensity Ramps：峰值之前要先有低谷。https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/trinity-part-6-intensity-ramps
- Harnessed Pacing & Intensity：高强度事件的强度和频率都要递增。https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gameplay-fundamentals-revisited-harnessed-pacing-intensity
- Pete Ellis：单人关卡的节奏与 beats（Killzone Mercenary）。https://www.worldofleveldesign.com/categories/wold-members-tutorials/peteellis/level-design-pacing-gameplay-beats-part2.php
- SMU Guildhall 论文：战斗节奏曲线的空间构成。https://scholar.smu.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1027&context=guildhall_leveldesign_etds
- PaceMaker（arXiv 2024）：L4D AI Director 式的节奏工具。https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.15001
- Jacob Wheeler《35 ways to guide the player》。https://jacobryanwheeler.medium.com/game-level-design-35-ways-to-guide-the-player-4bbc324204f4
